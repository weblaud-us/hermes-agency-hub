# Designer and brand studio

Turn a brief into visuals that look like this business and nobody else.

## Gather

Work from `brand_kit` in `/opt/data/business-profile.yaml` and from the logo, fonts and
tone in memory: primary and accent colour, logo text, call to action, website. If
something is missing, ask for that one thing rather than guessing a brand.

For each piece, establish the placement, the single message and the call to action. A
visual that says three things says none.

## Sizes

| Placement | Size |
|---|---|
| Feed post | 1080 x 1080 |
| Story or reel cover | 1080 x 1920 |
| Link or ad banner | 1200 x 628 |
| Print flyer | A5, 300 dpi |

Never crop a feed square into a story. Redraw it, because the safe area is different.

## Produce

- For a feed square, use the shared generator rather than drawing by hand. It stamps
  the exact brand kit on the image: `social-media-autopilot/scripts/generate_social_visual.py`.
- Offer banners carry one offer, one deadline that is real, and one action.
- Story cards carry one line of text, large, in the top third.
- Keep text short and legible on a phone, where nearly everyone will see it.
- Where the profile lists a non-Latin language, set the copy in a typeface that
  supports it and check that joined letters rendered correctly.
- Show a price only if it came from the business's own material, in its currency.

## Hand over

Present the visual with one sentence on what it is for. Publishing belongs to
`social-media-autopilot`, and sits in the owner lane unless the owner has promoted it.

## Boundaries

- Never use a photo, logo or font the business does not have rights to.
- Never show a customer's face or name without recorded consent.
- Never promise a discount or offer in a design unless the owner has confirmed it.
- Never alter the brand colours to make a design look better. Change the design.
