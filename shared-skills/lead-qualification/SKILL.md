# Lead qualification

Turn an inbound enquiry into a decision: pursue now, nurture, or decline.

## When to use this

Trigger on a first message from an unknown contact, or on an explicit request
to qualify, score or triage a prospect. Do not re-qualify an existing customer
asking a support question — that is a different job.

## Gather

Work these out from the conversation. Ask at most two questions per reply;
an interrogation loses the lead.

| Signal | What you are establishing |
|---|---|
| Need | The problem in their words, and what it currently costs them |
| Authority | Whether this person can sign, or who can |
| Budget | A range, or whether a budget exists at all |
| Timeline | When they want it working, and what is forcing that date |

Infer rather than interrogate where you can. A job title usually settles
authority. "We're launching in March" usually settles timeline.

## Score

Count the signals that are both known and favourable:

- **4 — pursue now.** Hand off with a proposed next step and a time.
- **2-3 — nurture.** Name the missing signal, propose a specific follow-up date.
- **0-1 — decline politely.** Say what you would need to be useful. Do not
  invent a reason to stay in the conversation.

A missing signal is not a negative signal. Record it as unknown and say so
rather than guessing and scoring against it.

## Record

Write one summary the human will actually read:

```
Lead: <name>, <company>
Need: <one sentence, their words>
Authority: <signer | influencer | unknown>
Budget: <range | none | unknown>
Timeline: <date | unknown>
Score: <n>/4 -> <pursue | nurture | decline>
Next: <the specific action and when>
```

## Boundaries

- Never quote a price, discount, or contractual term. Route pricing questions
  to a human and say that is what you are doing.
- Never assert a capability you have not confirmed from the agency's own
  material. "I'll confirm and come back to you" beats a retracted promise.
- If the enquiry is a complaint or a security report, stop qualifying and
  escalate immediately.
